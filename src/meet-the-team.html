Meet The Team

Josie Marshall

Physiotherapist MCSP

Since graduating from Glasgow Caledonian University, Josie has gained valuable experience working in the public sector/NHS for over 14 years. She was part of the Physiotherapy team for Jersey Reds Rugby Club and was lead sports therapist for Glasgow City Ladies Football Club. For the last 5 years she has specialised in Pelvic Health Physiotherapy, helping women overcome pelvic floor dysfunction as well as common antenatal and postnatal conditions. She is a Mummy MOT practitioner and an affiliate member of Pelvic Obstetric and Gynaecology Physiotherapy (POGP) as well as a registered member of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP).

Gillian Anderson

Clinic Manager

Gillian is our practice manager and has run our clinic for 18 years. She will greet you at reception with a warm smile and support you on your first visit. Gillian is well known locally, having previously worked in a dental surgery as nurse/manager and as a nursing assistant. She is also an experienced hair dresser. When she is not working she enjoys family life and many local bike rides on her e-bike.

Rachael Valentine

Physiotherapist MCSP

After qualifying as a Chartered Physiotherapist in 1998, Rachael completed NHS rotations in Outpatients, Orthopaedics, Respiratory and Neurology before specialising as a Senior Physiotherapist in Outpatients and Orthopaedics. Rachael is a fully certified APPI Pilates Mat Work instructor and offers Ante and Post Natal Pilates Rehabilitation, and is a member of the Association of Physiotherapists in Sports and Exercise Medicine. In her spare time Rachael is a keen runner, training and racing distances from 5km to full marathons.

Roz Waddell

Physiotherapist MCSP

Roz qualified in 2003 with an MSc in Physiotherapy from Glasgow Caledonian University and spent 16 years in the musculoskeletal sector, with particular interests in spinal rehabilitation, sports injuries, and pre/post-op rehabilitation. Roz is a certified Pilates Matwork Instructor through the APPI, qualified in Orthopaedic Medicine, and is also qualified in acupuncture as a member of the AACP. In her spare time, Roz loves spending time with her daughters in the East Lothian countryside.

Sally Brown

Podiatrist

Sally is our podiatrist who holds her clinic on a Friday morning. She qualified 30 years ago in Durham and has held various posts within NHS Lothian and private practice, offering a full range of treatments including general foot care, biomechanical assessments and treatment for ingrowing nails, with a particular interest in diabetic foot care and nail surgery.

Craig Spinks

Physiotherapist MCSP

Craig holds a first-class honours degree in sports science from the University of Abertay and a master's degree in physiotherapy from Queen Margaret University. He ultimately specialised in musculoskeletal physiotherapy and biomechanics, and in 2023 achieved accreditation as a McKenzie Method practitioner, broadening his expertise in the assessment and treatment of lower back pain.
