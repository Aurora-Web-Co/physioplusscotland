Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the security of any personal information you provide us with. This statement outlines how we ensure this and how we comply with legislation on data protection.

This Privacy Policy explains any areas of our website which may affect your privacy and personal details, how they are processed, collected, managed and stored, and how your rights under UK GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) are protected.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information from you when you contact us directly by phone or email, for example your name, email address, postal address and contact number. Any personal information collected is used only for the purpose of responding to your enquiry or providing the service you have requested.

Appointment bookings made through our online booking partner, TM3, are handled directly by TM3 under their own privacy policy — we recommend reviewing this when booking online.

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to review any personal information we hold about you at any time. This includes reviewing it for correctness, requesting correction of errors, requesting that it be deleted from our records, and opting out of communication. We do not sell, rent or share your details with any third party for commercial reasons.

2. Keeping Your Information Secure

Any data provided by you to us will be kept as secure as possible. We are fully aware of our responsibilities regarding data under UK GDPR. If we ask an external organisation to provide a service for us, we ensure that they have appropriate security measures in place.

3. Cookies

This website embeds a Google Map on our Contact page, which may set cookies from Google to provide map functionality. We do not use our own analytics or advertising cookies. Where you follow an external link from this site — for example to book an appointment via TM3, or to our Facebook page — that external site's own cookie and privacy policy will apply.

4. External Web Links

This website contains links to external websites, including our online booking system and our Facebook page. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site before submitting personal information to it.

5. Social Media Profiles

We maintain an official profile on Facebook. We advise our users to verify the legitimacy of any profile purporting to be us before interacting with it. We will never ask for your passwords or other sensitive personal details over social media.

6. Spam Policy

We are strongly against spam. We only contact you when you have requested to receive communication, or where there is a need to update you on services you have requested from us.

7. Other Privacy Notices

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the services requested, unless a longer retention period is required by law. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us. We may revise this policy from time to time; any changes will be posted on this page.

This Privacy Policy was last revised in July 2026.
