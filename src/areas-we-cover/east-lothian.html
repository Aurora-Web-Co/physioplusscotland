Physiotherapy in East Lothian

At Physiotherapy Plus, our team of Chartered Physiotherapists work across East Lothian, providing expert treatment for lower back pain, sciatica, and chronic back pain. Sciatica affects around 40% of the population and is most common in adults in their 40s and 50s — most cases improve within 4–6 weeks with the right treatment.

To help prevent lower back pain, stay active, avoid sitting for extended periods, take care when lifting, and try not to slouch when standing or sitting.
