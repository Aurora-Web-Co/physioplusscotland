Pilates

Why Pilates?

We now offer classes led by Physiotherapists and fitness instructors. Pilates is a form of low impact conditioning exercise that is safe and strengthens the core muscles of the whole body. It also improves general flexibility, promotes good postural alignment and helps you feel stretched, relaxed and lighter after each class.

Our physiotherapists are trained by the Australian Physiotherapy and Pilates Institute (APPI), who coach modified Pilates moves based on the original 34 moves Joseph Pilates created. It is suitable for almost everyone, including those with postural, spinal, musculoskeletal or neurological conditions. We also offer 1:1 or small group Pilates rehabilitation.

  • Strengthens and tones the core and body
  • Improves general flexibility, proprioception and balance
  • Ideal rehabilitation tool — relaxes and invigorates
  • Promotes wellbeing and good sleep
  • Safe and effective for sciatica, arthritis, osteoporosis, scoliosis, MS and many more
  • Restores muscle balance
